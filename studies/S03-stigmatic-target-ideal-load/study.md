# S03 — Shared-conjugate target and prescribed load

**Question.** What full-interface mean traction is needed to form a
shared-conjugate Cartesian pair, before restricting that load to acoustic
source commands?

**Protocol.** Construct the optical pair, preserve three phase volumes, derive
the load independently from continuous capillarity and gravity, and advance
both interfaces from flat under a fixed spatial load. Check mechanical and
fluid refinement and trace rays through the actual evolving surfaces.

**Verdict.** [S03-ideal-load-formation](../../STATUS.md) is a positive numerical
mechanics result for a declared three-fluid Stokes model with hypothetical
viscosities. It supplies a target load for the source-reachability question;
it does not show that any acoustic source can generate that load. See the
[formation report](../../artifacts/studies/S03-stigmatic-target-ideal-load/ideal-load-2026-09-23/viscous-fluid-refined/report.md)
and [derivation](../../docs/ideal-load-first.md).
